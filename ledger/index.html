Tax settings & workings
How it's calculated

Estimate only. 2026: tax class 1 scale (unchanged since 2025). 2028: class U scale from Bill 8676, adopted 28 Oct 2026 (0% up to €26,650, ten brackets). Indexation raises salary and the SSM. 7% solidarity surcharge (9% above €150k). Social: employee 8% pension + 3.05% health; self-employed 16% + 6.1% + 0.75% accident + mutualité; 1.4% dependency; base capped at 5× SSM. Forfaits €540 (salary) and €480 (special expenses). S-Pension deduction capped at €4,500/yr. Tax credits, the self-employed minimum base and advance lag are left out. In net view each income loses its own social contributions and a share of income tax in proportion to its taxable amount. Portfolio growth is shown on both sides: untaxed income, then reinvested. Category buckets

These buckets only drive the Mix line under Details (needs vs. wants); the budget bar uses the Plan's blocks. Food is healthy food, Treats is pleasure food. Any single entry or special can be moved to another bucket in its edit dialog; specials default to Luxury.
